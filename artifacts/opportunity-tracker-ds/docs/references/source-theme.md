# Source theme evidence

The source app's global theme is defined in
`artifacts/opportunity-tracker/src/index.css` after its base token layer.

## Extracted signals

- Background: fixed diagonal gradient from deep indigo `#181935` through
  violet `#292952` and `#4a4570` to dusty lavender `#7c6c9b`.
- Surfaces: translucent white at roughly 8%, a 25% cool-lilac border, blur
  and saturation, and a deep violet shadow.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond for headings; Plus Jakarta Sans for body and
  controls; headings use slight tracking and a soft lilac text shadow.
- Accent: pale gold `#f3e5ab`, used for primary actions, active emphasis,
  deadlines, progress highlights, icon borders, and focus glow.
- Supporting text: pale lilac body copy `#dcd6e8`, muted lavender `#a8a0be`,
  and bright heading text `#f4effa` / `#f8f5ff`.
- Fields: dark translucent violet, 8px corners, cool-lilac border, and a gold
  focus border with a restrained glow.
- Cards: 16px corners, translucent glass, blur, a thin border, and a soft
  shadow; hover adds a small lift and a warmer glow.
- Motion: short ease transitions, small hover lift, floating particulate texture,
  and reduced-motion fallback.

The design-system `light` mode is the source's softer dusk interpretation; its
`dark` mode deepens the atmosphere for the source's strongest visual treatment.
